# Surface: noroof.dev home (es at `/`, en at `/en/`)

Scope: the one-page landing in both locales, plus thanks and 404 pages in the same world.
Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: local (Uruguay) and international companies and startups, in equal measure, deciding whether to trust noRoof as a partner.
Action: the contact form (Formspree) is primary and the products are secondary (biz.noroof.dev and the others).
Proof: the three products the company runs itself, the industry experience, and the pillars. No client names, no team, no metrics.
Memorable moment: the product containers' doors swing open to show what each one carries.

## Direction contract

THESIS: noroof.dev is a container yard on the Montevideo quay. Every service, product and industry is cargo that is stacked, sealed and declared on a manifest. It refuses the agency template of a split hero over a grid of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is quay concrete (#dcdad3). The ink is steel charcoal (#1b211f). The cargo is corrugated container steel in logo green (#1fa83c, with shades). Paper is manifest stock (#f7f7f3) with dashed ruled rows. Display type is Big Shoulders Stencil; body type is Archivo. Red Hat Mono is used only for codes and manifest data. There is a single green seal, and it is the only accent and every action. A single quay line is the axis that every section aligns to.

STORY: The visitor sees a consultancy that builds *with* them and that ships and runs its own products. They see how the work moves through the yard stations, check the industry manifest, read the pillars (AI-driven engineering is named, not explained), then seal a message or open a product.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A dark gantry rail carries the nav, the ES/EN plates and the "Hablemos" seal. A 40-ft green container side covers about 70% of the width, with the stenciled 4-line headline and its ISO code in the corner. To its right, the door-end holds a bill of lading: a lead line, shipper and consignee rows, the AI pillar, a "Ver productos" link, and the green "Hablemos" seal as the primary action. Below the quay line sit the three product containers (Biz, Sport Club, Coolturus) with codes and status.

FORM: Montevideo port container yard, ISO 6346 markings and bills of lading. It is candidate 4 of my 7. Seed ea503897. Signature interaction: the container doors open in CSS 3D; with reduced motion they open without animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
